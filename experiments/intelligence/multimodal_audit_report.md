## 2. Audit of Fusion Implementation & Weight Distribution

### 2.1 Score Normalization & Fusion Logic
In `src/multimodal_classifier.py`:
* **Text Branch Normalization**: Raw composite scores $S_{\text{text\_raw}}(c)$ are Min-Max normalized across the 9 categories per image:
  $$\hat{S}_{\text{text}}(c) = \frac{S_{\text{text\_raw}}(c) - \min_k S_{\text{text\_raw}}(k)}{\max_k S_{\text{text\_raw}}(k) - \min_k S_{\text{text\_raw}}(k)}$$
* **Visual Branch Normalization**: TinyCLIP zero-shot prompt similarities $S_{\text{vis\_raw}}(c)$ are Min-Max normalized across the 9 categories per image:
  $$\hat{S}_{\text{vis}}(c) = \frac{S_{\text{vis\_raw}}(c) - \min_k S_{\text{vis\_raw}}(k)}{\max_k S_{\text{vis\_raw}}(k) - \min_k S_{\text{vis\_raw}}(k)}$$
* **Fused Score Calculation**:
  $$S_{\text{fused}}(c) = w_{\text{text}} \cdot \hat{S}_{\text{text}}(c) + w_{\text{vis}} \cdot \hat{S}_{\text{vis}}(c)$$

## 3. Investigation of Score Calibration & Normalization

### 3.1 Per-Image Min-Max Normalization Limitations
* **Why Selected**: Per-image min-max scaling maps heterogeneous score ranges ($S_{\text{text}} \in [0.0, 1.5]$ vs $S_{\text{vis}} \in [0.15, 0.35]$) onto a shared $[0.0, 1.0]$ boundary.
* **Limitations**:
  1. Per-image min-max scaling distorts relative confidence scale between images; a weak top score in an ambiguous image gets stretched to $1.0$.
  2. Softmax probabilities with temperature scaling or Platt scaling/Isotonic regression fit on the calibration set provide superior probability calibration.

## 4. Audit of Evaluation Metrics & Top-k Recall Behavior

### 4.1 Explanation of Top-2 Recall Equaling Top-1 Accuracy
In both Evaluation A and Evaluation B, **Top-2 Recall** equaled **Top-1 Accuracy** (84.52% / 142 of 168 screenshots for text-only):
* **Cause**: When `FinalRefinedClassifier` misclassifies a screenshot (Top-1 prediction $\neq$ Ground Truth), the true category is rarely in position #2; position #2 is occupied by fallback categories (`General` or `Work & Career`).
* **Position #3 Jump**: When evaluating `Top-3 Recall`, the true category is present in position #3, causing Top-3 recall to jump to **95.24%** (160 of 168 screenshots).

## 5. Audit of Latency Measurements

* **Text-Only Latency**: Pure rule + MiniLM inference takes **~1.8 - 2.5 ms** when models are pre-loaded in memory. When file/cache I/O overhead is included, latency measures **~24.6 ms**.
* **Visual Branch Latency**: TinyCLIP PyTorch forward pass on CPU takes **~105.0 - 119.5 ms** per image.
* **Recommended Benchmarking Protocol**: Measure warm inference execution time after pre-loading weights, reporting median and 95th-percentile latencies separately from disk I/O.

## 7. Trustworthiness Assessment & Conclusion

1. **Which Evaluation Results Are Valid?**
   * **Evaluation B** (saved on disk at `experiments/intelligence/multimodal_evaluation_report.md`) represents the accurate, reproducible baseline for the current implementation with default weights $w_{\text{text}}=0.70, w_{\text{vis}}=0.30$.
2. **Honest Empirical Finding**:
   * On text-rich screenshot datasets, **Text-Only classification (84.52% Top-1)** outperforms **Multimodal fusion (82.74% Top-1)** when visual features receive 30% weight, because zero-shot CLIP visual embeddings introduce visual container noise on text-dense screens.
   * Visual evidence provides value primarily when OCR text is sparse or absent ($w_{\text{vis}}=0.85$).
